# Forward scanner-selection measurement pilot — 2026-10-05

Status: engineering candidate, not activated. This is a measurement pilot, not a confirmatory performance test. No historical outcome search, new weights, paid provider, trading, service or scanner policy changes. The existing prospective model ledger remains independent and unchanged.

## Question and population
Can the current scanner feed produce contemporaneous eligible controls and sufficiently complete, identical 60-minute endpoints to support an honest selection test? Population is the scanner's observed, capped and filtered Pump/Solana candidate feed. It is not every Solana token. Current source selects at most 60 launches aged 30 minutes to six hours, with recent trade activity and its existing market-cap/ATH gate. Those gates are disclosed; provider ATH is never an outcome label.

## Fixed pilot rules
- Activation is an explicit future UTC timestamp bound to this protocol's SHA256. All tokens already known in launches, snapshots or V2 cases are excluded at activation. Historical tokens and pilot observations cannot enter a later confirmatory cohort.
- Enrollment stops exactly 14 days after activation, regardless of winners, losers, coverage, matches or apparent effect. Passive follow-up ends 3,780 seconds later. No sample-size target based on eligible outcomes and no extension after inspecting results.
- At each existing scanner cycle, capture every successfully computed full score vector together, before security review or Telegram. Capture admission failures and missing/provider inputs in a separate cycle-health record; do not silently treat a partial cycle as a complete universe. Cycles that span more than 120 seconds or contain invalid inputs fail as a whole and count as capture failures.
- Record exact local receipt/capture time, minute-bucket snapshot time, creation timestamp in seconds, pair, prior alert level, all 11 emitted signals, their score, basic eligibility fields and SHA256 of exact immutable decision rows. Creation dates are provider supplied; token age is not asserted as independently verified chain age. Retain raw whitelisted decision rows privately in the adapter for hash verification. Do not reconstruct emitted inputs later.
- Basic eligibility: at least four history rows spanning 600 seconds; market cap $30k–$750k, liquidity at least $25k, one-hour volume at least $20k. Eligibility is separate from the eight-point score threshold. The liquidity point is redundant with the gate and remains disclosed.
- An index event is the first score >=8 with prior production alert level zero for a new token. Retain events even when they lack a control or later fail screening. A higher-level repeat is not a new primary event.
- Controls are distinct tokens in the same complete evaluated cycle, basic eligible, score <8, no prior delivered alert, and not previously score-qualified in this pilot. Require age within 15 minutes and market cap/liquidity within a factor of two. Select at most three by ascending normalized age difference plus absolute log market-cap and liquidity ratios; break ties by mint string. No outcome, future alert, price momentum, regime, score-component similarity or wallet evidence may select controls. Later-alerting controls stay controls for the original event. Control reuse and tokens with both roles are reported explicitly.
- Score qualification, risk verdict and successful delivery are different events. Risk screening is not retroactively imposed on controls and the primary estimand is score qualification, not screened/delivered alert advantage. Delivered-alert results are descriptive and conditional; they cannot isolate wallet-screening value.

## Identical endpoints
Index time is the common completed-cycle capture time for both groups, before screening or delivery. Entry market cap comes from each exact local input, no more than 120 seconds old at index. Record and report those input ages. Future observations use actual local provider-response receipt times, the same pinned pair and positive finite market cap. The primary diagnostic is endpoint market cap divided by input market cap. Select the earliest valid receipt at index+3,600 through index+3,780 seconds inclusive, only after that window closes. An earlier observation, switched pair, lifetime peak or later backfill cannot substitute. Missing endpoints stay missing, not losses. This is an endpoint diagnostic without continuous-path coverage certification, and must not be pooled with the older coverage-qualified historical or model-ledger estimand. Market-cap ratios are not executable returns or realized profits. Provider knowledge-time/price staleness is unavailable and remains an explicit limitation.

Only observations already fetched by the production scanner or tracker may be passed to the pilot. No new HTTP/RPC request is issued by capture. Report differential fetch eligibility, pair availability and missingness by group, especially when the scanner stops fetching a deteriorating token. If passive response reuse yields differential or inadequate coverage, fail this measurement gate; do not claim an edge from surviving complete pairs. A symmetric bounded follow-up design would then need a separate reviewed change and provider-budget assessment before a confirmatory test.

## Pilot report and next research gate
At day 14 report cycle attempts/success/failure, eligible population, first qualification events, zero-control events, control reuse, available decision days, timing distributions, endpoint availability by group, and screening/delivery counts. Health monitoring may show operational counts during collection; do not expose effect estimates, win rates or outcomes before the fixed pilot stop. All effect summaries are exploratory after stop. The candidate core has no scheduler, reporter or outcome-blinding access-control integration yet; do not label this protocol activated or preregistered until those are reviewed and the activation receipt exists.

A later confirmatory protocol must be frozen before fresh observations: define primary paired mean log endpoint market-cap ratio difference, control aggregation/weights, UTC decision-day clustered inference and treatment of reused controls/mints; justify power against a stated commercially meaningful effect using pilot coverage/arrival rates and transparent variance assumptions. Define an outcome-independent deadline/sample cap, minimum matched decision days, missingness gate and sensitivity analysis before activation. Do not require a minimum winner count merely to analyze a continuous endpoint. No profitable-trading claim without a separate executable-cost/slippage test.

## Runtime integration gate
The current deliverable is a tested private SQLite event core and this pilot protocol. It is not wired into production. Before deployment, review adapters at `enrich_and_score` (capture exact common cycles), `alert` (separate screening/delivery receipt) and tracker `track` (reuse actual existing response receipts). Preserve existing ledger function bodies, model, configuration, queue and stop; no `pl_*` tables are touched by the pilot. Add a reviewed cycle-health record, runtime protocol/source binding, private input storage, fresh-token exclusion snapshot and count-only status endpoint. Validate identity/unit conversion, resource bounds, SQLite contention, failure isolation and identical passive-fetch provenance with fixture-based integration tests. Capture failures must pause pilot enrollment and be visible while the original alert service continues. Before activation, inspect a crypto release, review source hashes, create consistent backup and use the existing authorized deployment path. No unchecked root command or separate daemon is introduced.

## Security and storage
Only reviewed source/protocol/tests/aggregate handovers go to GitHub. The pilot database, exact input rows, identities, endpoint observations, exclusions and screening records live outside the checkout with private permissions. `capture.py` accepts a supplied SQLite connection and creates only `fc_*` tables. It has no import-time writes, provider client, production import, credential access, paid call or execution action. Tables reject updates/deletes; event chain is checked before writes and endpoint reads. This is local tamper evidence, not protection against a privileged server owner, external notarization or full deletion of a database. Scheduler activation, concurrency control, integrity stop/recovery and independent chain-head anchoring require integration review.
