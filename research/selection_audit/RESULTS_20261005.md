# Historical crypto alert-selection audit — 2026-10-05

**Result: scanner advantage remains unestablished. The retained first-SHADOW dataset does not support the planned matched comparison. Alert timing warrants a separate forward test.**

## 1. Question
Do alerted tokens outperform comparable tokens that were unalerted at the decision time? Also inspect whether historical alerts arrived during a quiet buildup and whether outcome coverage is adequate.

## 7. Matched comparison result
**0 matches from 132 alerts.** All failed to find a comparable stored SHADOW observation within the preceding five minutes, same regime, market cap and liquidity within a factor of two, with basic eligibility and no prior retained alert. Matching never consulted outcomes; later-alerting controls were permitted. No rule was relaxed after seeing zero matches. There is no paired effect estimate or significance claim. Unmatched means the planned comparison is unsupported, not that scanner edge is zero.

Unmatched raw summaries would be misleading. For context only, 291 SHADOW observations had coverage-qualified 60-minute endpoints, but only four SHADOW entries passed basic scanner eligibility. Comparing their pooled outcomes against ALERT outcomes would mix different entry conditions, ages and times.

## 8. Failed gate and limitations
The comparable-control gate failed. Stored first observations are not a time-aligned candidate/control cohort. Token-age matching is unavailable, exact emitted feature provenance is incomplete, and ALERT missingness is substantial. Old observations remain exploratory; market/time dependence and selection prevent causal or independent-token inference. This audit did not inspect or amend the active model test and did not perform model training or parameter search.

## 10. Next gate
Freeze a separate forward scanner-selection protocol, then review a minimal capture change using existing scanner inputs: exact contemporaneous eligible candidate set, alert versus non-alert admission, decision timestamp, token age, market cap/liquidity, emitted score/signals, pair and screening policy. Follow both groups from the same index-time rule and retain missingness. Capture score qualification separately from final screened/delivered alerts. Keep later alerts in the index-time control cohort under a predefined rule. Preserve the ongoing model-ledger cohort and do not refit or alter its stop based on this historical audit.

Before activation, define the primary 60-minute endpoint, matching/control-selection rules, decision-day clustered analysis, sample size/power and outcome-independent stop. Do not promise a verdict from a fixed few days of collection. The next useful build is this small measurement correction; additional agent/trading/model layers remain deferred until scanner selection can be evaluated.
